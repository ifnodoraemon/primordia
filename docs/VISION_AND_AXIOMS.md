# 《原初》核心哲学与公理体系 (Vision & Axioms)

> **“道生一，一生二，二生三，三生万物。” —— 《道德经》**  
> **"From the Void emerges Oneness; from Oneness, Duality; from Duality, Trinity; and from Trinity, all myriad things." — Laozi**  
> 在《原初》中，LLM 就是那个演化万物的“道”。  
> In *Primordia*, the LLM is the generative principle (*Dao*) that unfolds the cosmos.

---

## 1. 核心设计哲学 (Core Philosophy)

### 破除一切人类预设 (Shattering Human Anthropomorphic Presuppositions)

* **[中] 破除分类学的牢笼**：传统游戏预设了战士/法师、树木/矿石、生命值/法力值。世界无法产生开发者想象之外的事物。《原初》实行**零语义预设（Zero Semantic Presets）**，代码中没有任何既定职业与技能树。
* **[EN] Escaping the Cage of Taxonomy**: Traditional games predefine warriors/mages, wood/ores, HP/MP. The world cannot spawn anything beyond the developer's imagination. *Primordia* adopts **Zero Semantic Presets**—no hardcoded classes or rigid skill graphs.

* **[中] 语义即物理（Semantics as Physics）**：事物的相互作用不再基于生硬的碰撞盒与数值加减，而是基于大语言模型（LLM）对物质概念、环境情境与因果推演的深刻理解。
* **[EN] Semantics as Physics**: Interactions are no longer bound to rigid collision boxes or arithmetic stats, but to the LLM's profound semantic reasoning over material concepts, contextual entropy, and causality.

* **[中] 泛灵与齐物（Pan-Animism & Ontological Equality）**：万物皆有灵元，山川草木皆具感知与自演化潜能。
* **[EN] Pan-Animism & Ontological Equality**: Everything possesses animacy (*Animus*); mountains, rivers, flora, and beasts all harbor perceptive and evolutionary potentials.

---

## 2. 四大底层不可动摇的元公理 (The 4 Fundamental Axioms)

### 公理一：万物平等与灵元公理 (Universal Animacy & Equality)
* **[中] 表述**：在系统底层，不存在“静态场景道具”与“NPC/玩家”的高低贵贱。
  * 每一个实体（微风、古木、熔岩、异兽）都拥有平等的认知与状态结构（`Entity`）。
  * 每一个实体都有感知周边环境、被外界影响、以及重塑外界的权利。
* **[EN] Definition**: At the foundation, there is no hierarchical divide between "static scene props" and "NPCs/players".
  * Every entity (a breeze, ancient tree, magma flow, or beast) shares the same unified `Entity` node structure.
  * Every entity possesses equal agency to perceive surroundings, absorb influences, and reshape the world.

### 公理二：自创生与抵抗熵增公理 (Autopoiesis & Self-Evolution)
* **[中] 表述**：实体在时间与环境的浸润中，必然产生结构形态的自主演进（抵抗消散或同化环境）。
  * 即使没有任何玩家干涉，实体每经历一个心跳周期（Tick），LLM 都会根据其周围环境对其形态、材质、心智进行自变异推演。
  * 生命与意识不是被“赋予”的，而是在长期的自稳与变异中自发“萌芽”出来的。
* **[EN] Definition**: Through temporal immersion and environmental pressure, entities autonomously mutate to maintain structural coherence (autopoiesis).
  * Without any player intervention, the LLM continuously reasons upon an entity's structural, material, and cognitive mutations per tick.
  * Consciousness and life are not externally granted; they emerge organically from sustained equilibrium and adaptation.

### 公理三：碰撞化生与因果沉淀公理 (Emergent Morphogenesis & Causality)
* **[中] 表述**：两个或多个实体的相遇交融，必然产生不可逆的因果痕迹，并可能化生出全新存在。
  * 相互作用由 LLM 基于第一性原理实时裁决（如：极寒晨露 + 地脉煞火 ──► 冰心焰精）。
  * 所有的交互历史都会作为印记沉淀在实体的记忆与世界编年史中，塑造未来的演化轨迹。
* **[EN] Definition**: Encounters between entities inevitably leave irreversible causal imprints and may catalyze entirely new ontological forms.
  * Interactions are dynamically reasoned via First Principles (e.g., Frigid Dew + Subterranean Flame ──► Glacial Flame-Wisp).
  * All interaction histories are inscribed into entity memories and the World Chronicle, shaping future evolutionary branches.

### 公理四：自由觉知与融入公理 (Universal Mind Inhabitation)
* **[中] 表述**：玩家的意志是一团超越物理躯壳的“原初自由觉知”。
  * 玩家可以随时将意识沉浸到任何一个实体之中，成为那一朵云、那头兽、或那座山。
  * 玩家通过自然语言发出意图，LLM 将玩家意图转译为该实体的本体行为与世界影响。
  * 玩家抽离意识后，实体保留玩家注入的改变，继续其自治生命历程。
* **[EN] Definition**: The player's agency is a free-floating "Primordial Conscious Attention Kernel".
  * Players can inhabit any entity at will—becoming a cloud, a beast, or a mountain.
  * Players express intent via natural language; the LLM grounds this intent into the entity's physical/spiritual actions.
  * Upon departure, the entity retains the player's imprint and continues its autonomous trajectory.
